# ADR 0002: Undo and Redo, as Finder has them

Status: **accepted; implemented** (10 November 2026).

## Problem

In Finder, ⌘Z takes back what the person just did to their files: a move, a Move to Trash (put back where it was), a rename, a copy or a duplicate (the copy goes to the Trash), a new folder, a tag change; ⌘⇧Z does it again. The Edit menu names the exact step. Dropsea undid moves and copies only, from one history for the whole process, and an Undo that met a changed world did whatever part of it still could.

## Decision

1. **A window has its own history** (`UndoHistory`, `lib/undo`, held by `BrowserProvider`): kept while the window is open, through focus changes, and gone with it (a quit forgets it, as Finder's). It keeps the latest 100 steps. A new step forgets what was undone.
2. **A step is what the person did, however many items it took**, one Undo for all of them: a move (a drag, a drop from another application; a paste once the platform has a clipboard of files), a copy, File › Duplicate (Mod+D, Finder's `notes copy.txt`), a rename, New Folder, Move to Trash, Put Back, and a change of tags. Items of this device and Oxy Files' assets put in the Trash together are one step. Each item is an entry saying where it is now, and each entry has one opposite: a move or a rename goes back, what came to be (a copy, a duplicate, a new folder, an item put back) goes to the Trash, never deleted, an item in the Trash is put back, an asset is taken out of Oxy Files' trash or put in it, and tags go back to what they were. Redo is what an Undo did, taken the other way.
3. **Deleting for good is no step** (Empty Trash, Delete Immediately, Delete… where there is no Trash): the window asks first and says it cannot be undone.
4. **An Undo checks the world before it touches anything**, for every entry: the item still where the step left it, the folder it goes back into there, its name free there (case aside, as the file systems that fold case have it), an item still in the Trash, tags as the step left them. If anything differs, nothing changes: an alert says why in Finder's words (“The operation can’t be completed because an item with the name “notes.txt” already exists.”), and the step stays, to be undone once the person has cleared the way. Nothing is ever put over an item: the platform refuses to replace one. An entry the file system refuses after the check (another program raced it) stops the rest and what was already taken back is done again, so a step is undone whole or not at all.
5. **Everything goes through the platform:** moves and renames through `expo-file-system` (OxyOS's file service, with its capabilities), the Trash and Put Back through `PlatformFiles.trash` and `restore`, tags through `PlatformFiles.tags`, Oxy Files through the SDK and Dropsea's backend. No shell, no path of Dropsea's own. Undo and Redo wait their turn behind the window's moves and copies (`inTurn`, `lib/operations`).
6. **The Edit menu names the step, in Finder's words:** “Undo Move of “Facturas””, “Undo Move of 3 Items”, “Redo Rename of “notes.txt””; in Spanish, each step a noun with its article, “Deshacer el traslado de «Facturas»”, “Deshacer el traslado de 3 elementos”, “Rehacer el cambio de nombre de «notes.txt»” (amended 14 November 2026: the first wording dropped Finder's “of”). With nothing to undo it is a plain, disabled “Undo”. A text field with the keys keeps its own Undo, as a Mac's first responder.
7. **After an Undo or a Redo** the items it put back or placed in the folder the window shows are selected, the first focused, as Finder selects them, and the screen reader says what was done (“Undone: Move of “Facturas””, “Deshecho: el traslado de «Facturas»”).

## Not in this decision

- Cut and paste of files: the platform has no clipboard of files yet. A paste is a move or a copy, and records as one when it comes.
- A Replace's item in the Trash stays there when the move that replaced it is undone.
- Album changes (adding photos, renaming, deleting an album) and uploads and downloads.
